# Scoring Rules

The default criteria are Communication, Technical Skills, Problem Solving, Experience Relevance, and Overall Recommendation. Each accepts whole numbers 1–10 by default. Events may define positive criterion weights; omitted weights are treated as 1.

Per-criterion averages use all submitted values for that candidate and round to two decimal places for display/export. A submission overall is the weighted mean of its criteria. Candidate overall is the mean of submitted submission overalls, rounded only for display. Highest and lowest are based on unrounded submitted overalls.

Candidates rank by overall descending; equal values use average per-criterion score (descending), then candidate reference number, then name as deterministic tie-breakers. A locked candidate or locked event rejects inserts and updates. Otherwise judges may edit their own submission through their original active link.
